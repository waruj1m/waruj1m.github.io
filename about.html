About James

Software developer, AI automation contractor, and technical operator.

I’m James Colby, a UK-based contractor and consultant working across software delivery, AI-assisted automation, SEO, marketing systems, and practical technical problem-solving.

My background is broad because most useful commercial work crosses boundaries. I’ve spent the last decade working across software builds, automation systems, digital growth work, and operational tooling for businesses that need real execution rather than layers of agency theatre.

I’m most useful where the problem is partly technical, partly operational, and partly commercial — the kind of situation where someone needs to understand the system, simplify it, and then actually ship the work.

What I tend to help with

That usually includes custom software, web and mobile applications, workflow automation, AI-enabled systems, SEO and growth infrastructure, and technical consulting for messy delivery situations.

How I work

Directly, practically, and with a bias toward reducing friction. The aim is to understand the actual constraint, improve the system, and leave the business with something more useful and easier to operate.

Best fit

Best fit for founders, operators, and businesses that need one contractor who can move between technical delivery, automation, systems thinking, and commercial pragmatism without losing the thread.
